Welcome!

Welcome!

Welcome! To the Design an Inclusive Spaceship Collaborative Operation (DISCO)!

We are a collaborative design sprint event combining accessibility and speculative design to imagine inclusive futures!

If you are at a DISCO and looking for information about crew or ship components please go to our workshop section. Otherwise feel free to look through our website to learn more about the project!

Mission Statement

The goal of the DISCO is to introduce our participants to topics of accessibility and universal design in a way that is fun and playful. We want to challenge the idea that accessible design is “boring” or a daunting topic to learn. We use speculative design and science fiction to replace “it always has been” with “what if?”

If you wish to know more about the project please go to our Mission section.

Run Your Own - Workshop

The practice of access and inclusion is iterative, imperfect, and never truly done. Therefore, this project is ongoing.

This first toolkit version is a “high fidelity prototype,” to get at the feel of a DISCO kit. If you are still interested in running a DISCO go to our Workshop section if you are interested in running your own DISCO! And please fill our our feedback form and let us know how it went and what can be improved upon.

Jump To Top

We strive for all aspects of our toolkit to be as accessible as possible, whether our digital cards or this website. If anything proves inaccessible, please let us know in the accessibility portion of our feedback form form for this project, and we will do our best to address the issue.

All materials in the toolkit are living materials; timestamps in version histories evidence updates. Each version strives to stay updated on conversations in the communities DISCO represents and works with.
