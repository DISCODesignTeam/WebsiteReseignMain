Jump To Top

We strive for all aspects of our toolkit to be as accessible as possible, whether our digital cards or this website. If anything proves inaccessible, please let us know in the accessibility portion of our feedback form form for this project, and we will do our best to address the issue.

All materials in the toolkit are living materials; timestamps in version histories evidence updates. Each version strives to stay updated on conversations in the communities DISCO represents and works with.
